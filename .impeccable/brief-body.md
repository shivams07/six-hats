# Live board (and every screen that inherits it)

Scope: the whole web frontend. The live board proves the world; Home, Create, Join, Archive and the passcode gate inherit it.
Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: CleverTap engineering and product teams in hybrid sessions (laptops and phones, board often screen-shared). Facilitator runs the flow; participants write, vote, and leave with a decision.
Constraints: keep every feature, flow and fact; UI copy may be tightened (hat names, prompts and limits stay exact). Must not feel loud or distracting, and must not feel like dull corporate form-filling. Light and dark themes, 375 px to 1600 px, keyboard operable, hats never by colour alone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each hat runs as a secret ballot: everyone marks in private, marks stay sealed, and the room counts them open together at 0:00. Refuses the sticky-note retro board: coloured cards in columns, avatar stacks, soft-shadowed rounded tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Cool ballot-paper ground, never cream; black print ink; one indelible violet meaning yours, live, cast. Neutrals from one nine-step tonal ramp. Hat colours live only on hat badges, the open hat's panel and flow swatches. Archivo on a strict 1.2 scale, condensed tracked caps for field labels, every numeral tabular. Hairline rules, square corners, an 8 px grid. States as printed marks: hairline at rest, filled when chosen, struck when disabled, violet ring on focus. Dark theme is the counting room at night: ink ground, paper type, lifted violet.

STORY: A participant sees at once which hat is open, what to write, how long is left, and that nobody reads their words until the count. After the count they see every slip with its author, cast violet marks, and leave with a declared decision and owned actions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Running step at 1440 × 900. App bar with tally wordmark. Session bar: title, focus question, code, status, invite. Flow strip of numbered step cells, then Vote and Decision. Main column: the ballot frame (hat panel with icon, name and focus; prompt at 34 px; countdown near 100 px tabular over a violet time rule), facilitator toolbar on its foot, composer with Seal point, then your sealed slips packed two-up. Right 360 px: the Register with tally marks and counts (host only), presence dots, scribe tag.

FORM: Secret ballot and counting hall, number 4 of 7 on the ordered list. seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: the count. At reveal, sealed slips break their seal and open in a staggered cascade, about 200 ms each; counts are drawn as tallies everywhere (register, counted steps, vote results); a vote is a violet ink mark. Reduced motion shows the end state instantly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
